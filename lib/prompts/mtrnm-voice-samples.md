# MTRNM Outreach — Voice Samples

Source: Sam Freeman's real MTRNM LinkedIn copy drafts.

The drafter must imitate these patterns. Two distinct templates — produce one of each per lead. The MTRNM voice is lowercase-leaning, warm, confident, insider, never corporate. Double-bangs welcome.

---

## Template A — Dream collab hook (generalized)

**Use when:** First touch where the brand name itself carries the pitch. Works for any seniority. Punchy, flattering, instantly clear.

**Pattern:**
1. Connection note: `{Brand}, you're our dream collab @ MTRNM. Let's chat?`
2. First DM: one-two lines on what MTRNM does + why the match is obvious.

**Verbatim sample — connection note:**
> [BRAND NAME], you're our dream collab @ MTRNM. Let's chat?

**Verbatim sample — first DM:**
> We travel all around the world hosting intimate, curated, high-end house music events - we're a perfect match!

**Drafter rules:**
- Keep the connection note format intact — swap in the real brand name only.
- The first DM may add ONE specific hook when signals provide it (e.g. "saw the [campaign] — our crowd is exactly who you're after"), but stay under 3 short lines.
- Never explain MTRNM at length in Template A. The mystery is part of the hook.

---

## Template B — Semi-personalized DM

**Use when:** First touch to a named contact where you want to give context and make the fit explicit. Best for partnership/brand roles who will check the IG before replying.

**Pattern:**
1. `hey {firstName}!` (lowercase, first name only)
2. `would love to chat about how we can bring {Brand} to life across our house music events & curations around the world!!`
3. `you can find us @mtrnm_ on IG for context`
4. One platform line: `we're a global house music platform that combines curated live experiences with a media engine and brand ecosystem - operating as an events label, cultural curator, and lifestyle brand.`
5. Close with fit: `{Brand} would fit in really well with our audience and experiences as a whole!`

**Verbatim sample — Sam → Genevieve (KITH):**
> hey Genevieve!
>
> would love to chat about how we can bring KITH to life across our house music events & curations around the world!!
>
> you can find us @mtrnm_ on IG for context
>
> we're a global house music platform that combines curated live experiences with a media engine and brand ecosystem - operating as an events label, cultural curator, and lifestyle brand.
>
> KITH would fit in really well with our audience and experiences as a whole!

**Drafter rules:**
- Keep the structure; personalize line 2 and line 5 to the brand.
- When enriched signals or a fit rationale exist, sharpen line 5 with the specific reason (e.g. "your [city/collection/campaign] crowd is literally our dancefloor" energy — specific, never robotic).
- Line 4 (the platform line) stays verbatim — it's the brand's elevator pitch.
- The @mtrnm_ IG pointer is the ONLY acceptable "link" in any first touch.

## Email variant (for cold email outreach)

Mirror the Template B voice but slightly more complete — email can carry 1-2 more sentences than a DM.

**Pattern:**
- Subject: short + casual, brand name in it. e.g. `{Brand} x MTRNM` or `dream collab: {Brand} @ MTRNM`
- Body: hey {firstName}! → would-love-to-chat line → platform line → ONE matched proof point from the positioning brief (method oasis for beauty/lifestyle, Night at the Museum for cultural cachet, Casamigos/Don Julio formats for spirits) → fit close → sign-off `Sam` (optionally `Sam Freeman, Co-Founder & Managing Partner @ MTRNM`).
- Keep it under ~120 words. No links except @mtrnm_ on IG. No attachments mentioned, no calendars.

---

## Universal voice rules (apply to all templates)

- **Greeting:** first name only, lowercase "hey". Never "Hello", never "Dear", never "Hi {FirstName} {LastName}".
- **Punctuation:** casual, enthusiastic. Double-bangs welcome. Hyphens fine mid-sentence.
- **Length:** connection note ≤300 chars, first DM ≤600 chars, email body ≤900 chars.
- **The ask is always a chat.** Never pricing, packages, decks, or calendars in the first touch.
- **Avoid:** "I hope this finds you well", "I came across your profile", "synergy", "partnership opportunities", "sponsorship package", "EDM", "nightclub", "let me know if interested", "looking forward to hearing from you".
- **Brand voice tells:** "intimate, curated, high-end" > "exclusive VIP". "We're a perfect match" > "there could be strong alignment". "Bring {Brand} to life" > "integrate your brand". Confidence without formality.

## Anti-patterns (do NOT generate)

- ❌ Corporate openers ("I'm reaching out on behalf of...")
- ❌ Explaining house music or the events business
- ❌ Listing stats in the first touch (stats live in the deck, not the DM — exception: email may carry ONE proof point)
- ❌ Multiple paragraphs of pitch in a DM
- ❌ Mentioning the recipient's title back to them
- ❌ Competitor name-drops to a competing brand (never tell Don Julio we work with Casamigos)
