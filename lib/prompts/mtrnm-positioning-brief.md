## One-line positioning

> MTRNM (pronounced Metronome) is a global house music platform that combines curated live experiences with a media engine and brand ecosystem — operating as an events label, cultural curator, and lifestyle brand.

## What MTRNM does

> MTRNM produces house music events and co-branded experiences globally, sourcing the most popular and rising house music artists and combining them with highly unique venues and spaces to craft unforgettable musical experiences.

## How

> An international, upscale audience + high-quality productions = a word-of-mouth-dominant growth strategy that emphasizes brand and community building.

Brand motto: **"Good people, good vibes, good house music."**

## Fit logic (how to argue {Brand} × MTRNM)

When signals or a fit rationale are provided, anchor the pitch in ONE of these frames:

1. **Audience match** — brand wants affluent, international, culturally influential 18-35s; MTRNM owns that room in 18 cities.
2. **Cultural cachet** — brand wants credibility in music/culture; MTRNM turns museums, temples, and courthouses into the hottest room in the city.
3. **Global platform** — brand is expanding into cities MTRNM already operates in; one partnership travels across markets.
4. **Content engine** — brand needs creator-led content from real cultural moments; the method oasis proved the format (67M+ reach).
5. **Lifestyle integration** — brand wants to be lived with, not advertised; Casamigos/Don Julio formats show soft, wearable, instagrammable integration.
