## Vocabulary to AVOID (off-brand)

- "Influencer marketing campaign" (use "student creator program" or "campus activation")
- "Reach out" alone (too generic — use a specific tactic)
- "Synergy", "leverage" (when used corporate-style), "best-in-class", "ROI", "KPI" in first touch
- "Partner up" (too cold)
- "Brand awareness" (too marketing-deck) — say "presence" or "buzz" instead

## Seasonality + back-to-school 2026 framing

Today is 2026-06-06. Back-to-school season runs roughly **August 15 – September 30, 2026**. The first-touch window for activation conversations to land in time for fall is **June–July 2026**. After mid-July, brands' fall budgets are typically locked.

When relevant, the drafter can reference:
- "Fall '26 campaigns"
- "Back-to-school activations"
- "Rush week" (varies by school but generally late August / early September)
- "Football season tailgates"
- "First semester ambassador program"
